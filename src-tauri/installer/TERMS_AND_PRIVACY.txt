3. PRIVACY POLICY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
YOUR WORK IS PERSONAL. HERE IS HOW WE HANDLE IT.

Folio is designed around a personal, device-isolated, and private workspace:

A. YOUR ACCOUNT AND PROFILE
Folio Desktop stores your display name, email address, sign-in provider
identifiers, and the personal rhythm preferences you configure. Optional
gender, country, and forms of address are used solely for your profile and
greeting personalization, never for eligibility, tracking, or advertising.

B. YOUR WORKSPACE AND LOCAL DATA
Projects, tasks, subtasks, milestones, ideas, notes, activity history, and
saved filters are stored securely in local SQLite (folio.db) on your machine.
When connected, data synchronizes privately over encrypted HTTPS/TLS with the
Folio cloud backend. Backend requests are strictly scoped to the authenticated
user ID.

C. DEVICE-ISOLATED SESSIONS
Signing in on Folio Desktop authenticates only this desktop application using
secure token-based Bearer authentication stored in your local application database.
Your web browser sessions remain completely separate and independent.

D. FOLIO AI AND PRIVACY
Folio AI only routes prompts when explicitly requested by you, using your
configured API key. Your API keys are kept on your device and are never shared
across accounts. Do not include passwords or sensitive personal identification
in AI prompts.

E. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION
You maintain full ownership of your data. You may export your workspace data at
any time through Settings -> Account. You can delete individual items or your
entire account directly from the Danger Zone in Settings, which permanently
erases all records from both your local database and the server.

F. UNINSTALLING FOLIO DESKTOP
Uninstalling Folio Desktop is always in your control:

  Windows — the uninstaller first closes a running Folio instance, removes the
  application, its shortcuts and its registry entries, and then asks whether
  you also want to delete your LOCAL workspace data. Choosing "No" keeps your
  data for a future reinstall; choosing "Yes" permanently removes
  %APPDATA%\com.folio.workspace (the folio.db local workspace database and
  cached session) and the WebView2 profile under
  %LOCALAPPDATA%\com.folio.workspace.

  Linux — "apt remove folio" (or your package manager's equivalent) removes
  the application while keeping your local data. "apt purge folio" removes
  the application together with ~/.config/com.folio.workspace and
  ~/.local/share/com.folio.workspace.

  macOS — drag Folio from Applications to the Trash to uninstall. Your local
  data remains in ~/Library/Application Support/com.folio.workspace and may be
  deleted from there at any time.

Nothing is deleted silently: the local workspace data is only removed when you
confirm it during uninstall (Windows), purge the package (Linux), or delete
the data folder yourself (macOS).
